About

I'm Mara, a designer working between brand and product. I've spent ten years making identities that have to survive contact with real interfaces, and interfaces that still feel like the brand.

Before going independent in 2021 I was a senior designer at Kurppa Hosk in Stockholm, and before that at BVD.

I studied Visual Communication at HDK-Valand (2011–2014). Kvarn Sans won a Type Directors Club Certificate of Excellence in 2024.

Clients

Fjord Coffee, Tessel, Göteborgs Stadsbibliotek, Salt Festival, Loppis, Volvo Cars (via Kurppa Hosk), IKEA (via BVD)

Services

  • Product & interface design

  • Design systems
