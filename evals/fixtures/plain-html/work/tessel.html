Tessel

Product design · 2024 · Lead product designer

Tessel is a scheduling app for physiotherapy clinics. I designed the 1.0 app and its design system over eight months.

Context

Clinics were running on paper diaries and a shared Google Sheet. The team had a working prototype and no designer.

Approach

Two weeks shadowing reception desks, then a calendar built around the 45-minute appointment rather than the hour.

  • Booking time cut from 3 minutes to 40 seconds in testing
  • Design system of 60 components in Figma

Engineering: Tessel team · PM: Lina Haddad
