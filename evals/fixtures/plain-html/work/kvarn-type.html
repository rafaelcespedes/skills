Mara Lindqvist

Kvarn Sans

Type design · 2023 · Designer

A grotesque with a mill-stone curve in the bowls, drawn over three years of evenings. Six weights, Latin Extended.

Kvarn started as the wordmark for Fjord Coffee and grew into a family. It won a Type Directors Club Certificate of Excellence in 2024.
