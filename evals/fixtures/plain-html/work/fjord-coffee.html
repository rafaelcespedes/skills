Mara Lindqvist

Fjord Coffee

Brand identity, Packaging · 2024 · Design lead

A new identity for a small Gothenburg roastery moving from one café to supermarket shelves.

The problem

Fjord had a hand-drawn logo that fell apart below 2 cm. They needed a system that worked on a 250 g bag, a cup sleeve and a shelf talker.

What we made

A wordmark cut from Kvarn Sans, a tidal colour system keyed to each origin, and a modular bag layout that the roasters fill in themselves.

Sales in the first quarter on shelf were 40% above forecast.
